# Audit decision projections

Human and `decisions.jsonl` projections now expose bounded facts from `dataset_diagnosed`, `validation_planned`, `postprocessing_selected`, and `trial_completed`. Dataset output reports adversarial ROC-AUC (including the temporal-excluded score and measured time when present), future-only temporal ranges, and shift counts/rates without category values. Validation plans retain support policy, fold count, coverage, and at most five warnings. Postprocessing shows acceptance, up to three member trials and weights, class multipliers, the baseline/assessed/calibrated scores, and a short assessment. Trial diagnostics cap class rows at 8, confusion matrices at 10×10, regression segments at 5, and feature importance at 8; terminal text summarizes only the worst few facts.

The new tests first failed on all four requested projections. After implementation, `tests/test_research_audit.py` and the existing `tests/test_audit.py` passed (9 tests). Projections use per-event field allowlists and typed bounded values; private keys and unrecognized arrays/text are omitted. No preflight event is projected or invented because no canonical preflight event contract exists.
